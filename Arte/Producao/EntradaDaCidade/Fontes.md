# Arte da Entrada da Cidade

Geração com a ferramenta ImageGen integrada e com a picape da Rua Norte como referência visual. `Prancha-ImageGen.png` e `Mala-ImageGen.png` são as fontes de geração. `ObjetosEntrada.aseprite` contém a folha editável de produção a 64 pixels por unidade. O jogo importa uma única folha recortada em cinco sprites, com pivôs na base.

## Prompt da prancha

Create a single transparent 2x2 SPRITE SHEET for a rainy Brazilian small-town pixel-art game set in 2004. Image 1 is the game's approved pickup sprite, a style and camera reference only. Give the four assets ample completely transparent gutters so each quadrant can be cut out independently. No grid lines, swatches, labels or stage. Render all in exactly the supplied grounded, dense, finely dithered 64px/unit pixel-art style: lots of subtle clustered material details, weathering, restrained bevels, naturally uneven dark outlines and deep texture. Match the elevated side-on/front-on view (top surfaces visible, no strong isometric slant). Top-left: a dark tobacco / reddish brown early-1990s four-door sedan, side-on nose left like the pickup, old dull dark brown paint, rain-worn body, dark glass, dusty hubcaps, believable tires and early-90s rectangular lights. Distinguish enclosed sedan trunk and rear window from pickup. Its intended in-game size is 270x121 pixels (wide low car). Top-right: small-town entrance sign, two separated aged timber posts supporting a wide framed dark bottle-green painted sign, body facing straight to camera. The sign reads EXACTLY "SANTA RITA" line 1 and "JAMAIS ABANDONA" line 2 in hand-painted weathered ivory block letters. Its in-game bounds about 225x164 pixels. Bottom-left: municipal bus-stop bench viewed elevated straight-on, rain-darkened wood slats with subtle wood grain, dark greenish cast iron frame and two pairs of simple short legs. Size about 129x65 pixels. Bottom-right: weathered wooden bus stop noticeboard with narrow green "PARADA" header, shallow galvanized rain cap, two separate posts, pinned yellowish paper scraps including a bus schedule. Size about 99x120 pixels. All objects touch transparent bottom ground at their feet, no baked cast shadow, no floor, no scenery, no ambient halo or glow. Each distinct object fully contained inside its own quadrant; genuinely transparent canvas. Focus on a cohesive professional game sprite sheet, materials and density like the reference, rather than flat vector illustration.

## Prompt da mala

A single transparent game sprite of an old open empty suitcase, viewed front-on from the slightly elevated top-down perspective of a 2D exploration game set in a small Brazilian town, 2004. The attached pickup sprite is a reference for the in-game finely textured pixel-art material and scale, no vehicle in the result. The suitcase is a small scuffed boxy 1980s hard-shell travel case, dark worn ochre brown shell, old oxidized corners and latch, restrained gray-brown fabric lining visible inside the empty open body, lid tilted back, a tiny torn off-white bus luggage sticker. Place hinge at the far edge; little handle at the near edge. Silhouette must immediately read as an OPEN abandoned suitcase from gameplay distance. One object on genuine transparent alpha, centered, full perimeter with margin, no ground, no mud, no grass, no halo, no cast shadow. Rich clustered pixel detailing and restrained highlights like the reference. Planned final size about 66 x 55 pixels at 64 pixels per game unit.
